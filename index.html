The format stops being a surprise

The PSAT math section is 44 questions in two 35-minute modules, with a graphing calculator on screen throughout. Every practice test here runs the same way, so October is the sixth time you have done it rather than the first.

  • Two timed modules, 22 questions each. The clock behaves the way it will on the day.

One honest thing about National Merit

The Selection Index is calculated as twice the Reading and Writing score plus the Math score, divided by ten. Math is roughly a third of it, and only the eleventh grade sitting counts. This plan will move a math score properly. It will not, by itself, make a student a Semifinalist, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling something.

Questions people ask

When is my PSAT?

Your school picks the day, anywhere from October 1 to October 30. You do not register yourself. Ask your counselor for the exact date, and if you cannot find out, start anyway. The plan works from wherever you begin.

Is this the same as the SAT plan?

The plan is different. It runs thirty days aimed at October and it opens with four days of Foundations. The practice questions are drawn from my SAT bank, because the PSAT and SAT test the same four content areas and the same skills. The SAT reaches a little higher at the very top end, so if anything you will be practising slightly above what you meet in October.

How accurate is the practice score?

Every practice test reports an estimated PSAT math score on the real 160 to 760 scale. Treat it as a direction rather than a prediction. The questions are drawn from my SAT bank, which runs slightly harder at the top end than the PSAT does, so the estimate tends to come in below the real thing rather than above it. Nobody can promise a score, and any prep company that does is guessing.

How long does a day actually take?

Approximately thirty minutes for most days. The four Foundations days at the start run longer, and the seven full tests need about seventy minutes each, so plan for those separately. If you can only give it fifteen minutes, it takes longer than thirty days and that is fine. Nothing expires.
