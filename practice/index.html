Before you start

Get a penciland some paper.

This matters more than anything else on this page

Do not do this in your head, and do not stare at the four choices waiting for one to look right. Write the working out on paper for every single question.

Students who click through quickly get almost nothing from a practice set, and the score they get is a measure of their guessing, not their math. Working on paper is slower and it is the whole point.

On test day you get scratch paper too. Practising the way you will sit the test is how the habit holds up under time pressure.

How this works Untimed
  • There is no clock. Take as long as each question needs.
  • Aim for at least two minutes on a question before you answer it.
  • You will not be told right or wrong as you go. Everything is reviewed at the end, which keeps it practice rather than a quiz.
  • The calculator and formula sheet are there throughout.
  • Cross out choices you have ruled out. It is what you would do on paper.
