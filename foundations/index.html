Days 1 to 4

Foundations.The floor under everything else.

Most PSAT points are not lost on hard questions. They are lost on ordinary questions sitting on a shaky basic. These four days rebuild the basics before anything gets measured. Work them in order.

These days run longer than thirty minutes. That is deliberate, and it is the part most prep skips.

How to work through a day

Read each concept, then answer the check question underneath it. If a check question stumps you, read the concept again and try the practice problems before moving on.

Your progress saves automatically to your account, so you can stop halfway and pick it up on any device.
