What the thirty days hold

Days 1–4

Foundations

Fractions, ratios, percentages, linear equations, exponents. Four days rebuilding the floor before anything is measured.

Days 5–6

First diagnostic

Forty-four questions in two timed modules, then a review naming every error and the skill underneath it.

Days 7–11

The four content areas

Algebra, Advanced Math, Data Analysis, Geometry and Trigonometry, weighted the way the real test weights them.

Days 12–13

Desmos

Twenty-four walkthroughs on which questions the on-screen calculator collapses into seconds, and which it slows down.

Days 14–22

Second diagnostic, then your own gaps

A fresh test with questions you have not seen, then days aimed at whatever your results say is weakest.

Days 23–30

Third diagnostic, then four full tests

The gap list should be visibly shorter. The last stretch is one timed test a day, then honest advice about the final night.
